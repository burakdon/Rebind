    How to work it

    Chart

    Only right-side rows are charted, numbered at the right, and read from right to left. Start at the bottom of each sheet. Each square is one stitch in the yarn shown. O is a yarn over, / is knit two together, an empty square is knit. The mark at the left says how to work the row back: p means k3, purl to the last 3, k3; k means knit every stitch. A Roman numeral marks the first passage of a chapter.

    Written instructions

    The same pattern in words. Each line is one passage of the book. The brackets name it and give the count of its sentence labels, which is where the yarn and the ridge come from; “balance” in the yarn table is warm labels minus cold ones, divided by the number of labels.
