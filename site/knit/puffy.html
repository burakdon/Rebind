        Row 1 is the first row you work after casting on. Loop 1 is at the left as you look at the front. Puffy is not turned, so you work one row leftwards and the next rightwards: read a row in the direction you are working it. An empty box is a normal loop, • is a reverse loop, and >< is a crossed pair.
